⚙️ Problem 3.1 — Therapeutic Toy Design

Goals

  • Apply everything from the unit to design a therapeutic toy for a child with cerebral palsy.
  • Develop a Design Brief for your chosen child and incorporate universal design.
  • Build, test, revise (v1 → v2), and evaluate your prototype.
  • Present your design to the class and the occupational therapist.

A real client. PLTW frames this around a fictional occupational therapist. In our class, a real occupational therapist visits twice — once to help you define the problem (Nov 13) and once to evaluate your final toy (Dec 9). Design for a real person.

🖨 Print deadlines matter. Your first prototype parts must be exported by Friday, Nov 20 — before the nine-day Thanksgiving break. Your revised (v2) parts export Friday, Dec 4.

➡️ Procedures

Follow the full procedures in myPLTW to complete Problem 3.1 Therapeutic Toy Design. (paste your myPLTW link here)

Key steps in this activity:

  1. Choose a child profile; with the occupational therapist's help, write the Design Brief (problem, client/user, criteria & constraints).
  2. Brainstorm and sketch ideas. Use the weighted decision matrix for your assigned child to select a concept.
  3. Create dimensioned multiview sketches, then model the toy in Tinkercad.
  4. Export and print v1; build and test it against the Design Brief.
  5. Analyze the results, revise the model, and print v2 (keep universal design in mind).
  6. Complete final assembly and present to the occupational therapist for evaluation.
