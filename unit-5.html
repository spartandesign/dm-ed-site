Unit 5 · Weeks 14–18

Capstone:
Skate Park

Everything comes together. Twelve teams of three design and build one connected skate park — each section printed to a shared connection standard so the pieces actually fit together. The capstone is graded as one project worth 100 points, and the grade closes this fall.

Week 14 Nov 9–13 · Brief + connection standard

Capstone Launch

Your team gets the brief and agrees on a connection standard — the shared peg size, spacing, and clearance every section must use so they all connect.

You will be able to

  • Understand the brief: 12 teams, one connected park.
  • Agree a connection standard (peg = hole − clearance) with your team.
  • Claim your section and sketch the idea.
  • Record the standard so every teammate builds to it.

A connection standard is tolerance for a whole class: if everyone uses the same peg-and-clearance, twelve separately-built sections snap together.

Capstone Connection standard + section plan (part of 100 pts)
Week 15 Nov 16–20 · Section models + drawing

Capstone Design

You model your section to the standard and produce an assembly drawing. Files are due before Thanksgiving so they print over the break.

You will be able to

  • Model your section at real scale, designed for printing, within the 180 mm bed.
  • Build the connection features exactly to the team standard.
  • Produce a section + assembly working drawing.
  • Submit print-ready files before the break.

Each section caps at 180 mm so it fits the printer bed; the connectors use the clearance rule so neighboring sections mate. Thanksgiving is the main print window.

Capstone Section models + drawing (part of 100 pts)
Week 16 Nov 30–Dec 4 · Fit check

Fit & Iterate

Printed sections come back from the break. Your team fits them together, finds what doesn't mate, and iterates.

You will be able to

  • Caliper the printed connectors and test the fit between sections.
  • Diagnose interference (too tight) or slop (too loose).
  • Adjust the model and reprint only the parts that need it.
  • Log each change and why you made it.

This is the tolerance spine's payoff — real parts, real fit, real iteration, across a whole class's worth of sections.

Capstone Fit-tested section + change log (part of 100 pts)
Week 17 Dec 7–11 · Protected buffer

Assemble

Teams join their sections into the full park and solve the last connection problems. This week is a protected buffer for reprints and fixes.

You will be able to

  • Connect your section to your neighbors' to the shared standard.
  • Solve last-mile fit issues across team boundaries.
  • Finish at least one printed, fit-tested section per team.
  • Prepare the park for the showcase.

The buffer exists because real builds run long — and because the same students return in spring to finish any remaining sections.

Capstone Assembled, connected sections (part of 100 pts)
Week 18 Dec 14–18 · Showcase; grades close

Showcase & Reflection

The class shows off the finished park. You reflect on tolerance, iteration, and what your team would change.

You will be able to

  • Present your section and how it connects to the whole park.
  • Explain a tolerance decision your team made, and why.
  • Reflect on what worked, what didn't, and what you'd change.
  • Close out your OneNote portfolio for the semester.

One fingerboard became a whole skate park — and the same idea, tolerance, runs from your Week 1 caliper reading to this final connection.

Capstone Showcase + reflection — completes the 100-pt capstone
