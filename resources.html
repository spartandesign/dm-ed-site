Documentation

How students document their work

Students keep two records that feed a third. The paper engineering notebook is where thinking happens — sketches, raw caliper readings, quick math. OneNote is the curated portfolio: cleaned-up evidence for each deliverable. Canvas is where the graded version is turned in. The notebook feeds OneNote; OneNote feeds Canvas.

The four-part OneNote page

Every deliverable lands as one OneNote page on the same template: the design (drawing or CAD screenshot), the made part (a photo, caliper in frame), designed vs. measured (the numbers), and a short reflection (what you'd change).

The engineering notebook

Set it up in Week 1 — numbered pages, a table of contents, dated entries — and keep it all semester. It's the one rail students never hand in; it's for thinking, not neatness. The polished version of any entry gets copied into OneNote.
