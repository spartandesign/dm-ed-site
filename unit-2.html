Unit 2 · Weeks 4–7

CAD in Onshape

You move from paper to the screen — the first time the computer, not a caliper, holds your dimensions. You'll learn parametric CAD in Onshape, print your first real part, and design a skateboard you'll later shrink into your fingerboard.

Week 4 Aug 31–Sep 4 · Onshape Unit 1

Welcome to Onshape

You learn parametric CAD by working through Onshape's Intro to CAD (lessons 1.1–1.4) on a shared model.

You will be able to

  • Navigate the Onshape interface and set your workspace units to inches.
  • Sketch, extrude, and edit features to build a simple part.
  • Check a part for interference — where two things overlap.
  • See how a 2D drawing comes from a 3D model.

Set your units to inches first — same as your calipers. A measurement bridge entry ties an on-screen dimension to a real caliper reading, so CAD and the physical world stay connected.

CAD Onshape Unit 1 (1.1–1.4) + measurement bridge (45 pts)
Week 5 Sep 7–11 · Cube + accuracy log

First Print: Calibration Cube

You model a 2.000 in cube, your teacher prints it, and you measure how close the machine actually got.

You will be able to

  • Model a precise 2.000 in cube and export it for printing.
  • Measure the printed cube on every axis with calipers.
  • Calculate deviation: measured size minus designed size.
  • Explain why a real machine never makes exactly what you draw.

This is where tolerance begins — the gap between the size you asked for and the size you got. The whole capstone is built on managing that gap.

Print Calibration cube + accuracy log (20 pts)
Weeks 6–7 Sep 14–25 · Onshape Unit 2

Designing a Skateboard

Over two weeks you work through Onshape Unit 2 (2.1–2.4) and design a skateboard — the model you'll shrink into your fingerboard deck.

You will be able to

  • Build a multi-feature part with sketches, extrudes, and a revolve.
  • Use mates and degrees of freedom to assemble parts.
  • Place mounting holes where the hardware actually goes.
  • Explain why hole placement decides whether the trucks fit.

Mates and revolves are the exact skills the capstone teams use to snap park sections together and shape ramps and bowls.

CAD Onshape Unit 2 (2.1–2.4) + bolt-hole note (45 pts)
