The hero object

One fingerboard, all semester

96 × 32 mmMETRIC DECK STANDARD

Every project points at the same object: a working fingerboard with real trucks and wheels. The deck is modeled to the 96 × 32 mm metric standard on purpose — it forces the inch→millimeter conversion (1 in = 25.4 mm) to become real, not abstract. Then twelve teams scale the idea up into one connected skate park.

The lab

Four machines, one operator

Your teacher operates every machine; you design and measure. Two lasers cover different jobs — knowing which one to reach for saves material and time.

Which laser?

  • Clear or colored acrylic → LC40 only. The Ador's diode wavelength can't cut it.
  • Wood, plywood, leather, card, or dark material → either machine.
  • Thicker or faster cutting → LC40.
